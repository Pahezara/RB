# ASSETS.md — asset register
Feeds `scripts/placeholders.mjs`. A row is satisfied when its status starts with
`HAVE` or `N/A`. Anything else blocks a production build.

`N/A` means the thing was deliberately dropped by a recorded decision, not forgotten.
Each one names the decision so it cannot quietly turn back into an oversight.

Every row below was checked against the client's own supplied documents on
2026-08-31.
